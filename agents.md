## 2. Project Overview & Data Architecture

- **Dataset**: UCI Bike Sharing (Hourly) (`data/hour.csv`, $N = 17,379$, 17 columns)
- **Unit of Observation**: Count of bike rentals in one specific hour in Washington, D.C. (Jan 2011 – Dec 2012)
- **Target Variable**: `cnt` (Total hourly bike rentals)
- **Component Variables (Dropped from Features)**:
  - `casual`, `registered` (Sum exactly to `cnt`; dropped to prevent target leakage, though examined in EDA)
  - `instant`, `dteday` (Row index and date string; redundant with temporal features)
  - `atemp` (Feeling temperature; excluded due to near-perfect collinearity with `temp`, $r = 0.9873$, $\text{VIF} > 300$)
- **Train / Test Split Strategy**:
  - **Chronological Holdout**:
    - **Training Set**: 2011-01-01 to 2012-09-30 ($N = 15,211$, 87.5%)
    - **Holdout Test Set**: 2012-10-01 to 2012-12-31 ($N = 2,168$, 12.5% — final quarter of 2012 held out)
  - **Rationale**: Adjacent hours exhibit strong temporal autocorrelation ($\rho_1 \approx 0.88$, $\rho_{24} \approx 0.70$). Random splitting causes data leakage and overly optimistic test error.
